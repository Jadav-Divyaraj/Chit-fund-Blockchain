🗺️ The 12-Feature Roadmap

F1 📱 PWA + Offline Mode Tier 1

Transform the web app into an installable Progressive Web App with full offline support. Tribal villages have intermittent 2G/3G connectivity — this makes the app usable in a moving van, on a hill, in a forest clearing.

Stack: Workbox · IndexedDB · Service Workers · Background Sync API

↑ 10x reach into remote villages
F2 🌐 IPFS Document Vault Tier 1

Store claim PDFs, photos, signatures, and tribal maps on IPFS. Only the content hash is stored on-chain (gas savings). Documents become censorship-resistant and verifiable forever.

Stack: Pinata / web3.storage · IPFS CIDs · content addressing

₹ 0 vs ₹500+ for centralized cloud
F3 📲 Web3 Push Notifications Tier 1

Replace SMS with EPNS — gasless push notifications. A family gets pinged the moment their payment lands, even if they never open the app. Also enables field volunteers to receive dispute alerts.

Stack: EPNS protocol · encrypted channels · mobile SDK

SMS 1M = ₹1L vs EPNS 1M ≈ free
F4 🎖️ Soulbound Claim NFT Tier 2

Mint a non-transferable ERC-721 NFT to each enrolled family as an on-chain certificate. Becomes a self-sovereign proof-of-claim that works offline, in any court, across any chain.

Stack: ERC-721S (soulbound) · SVG on-chain · LayerZero OFT

Family becomes own legal custodian
F5 🏛️ Cooperative DAO Tier 2

Upgrade the central admin multisig to a full on-chain DAO. Members vote on treasury allocation, fee changes, dispute policies. Transparent, forkable, and exit-friendly.

Stack: OZ Governor · Tally UI · Snapshot (gasless voting)

Removes "single point of trust"
F6 🔍 Public Verifier Portal Tier 2

A read-only website (no wallet) where journalists, courts, RTI activists, and NGOs can paste a transaction hash or family ID and instantly verify the on-chain claim. Transparency for the public.

Stack: Public RPC (rate-limited) · subgraph · embeddable widget

Media & court-friendly evidence
F7 🤖 AI Underpayment Detector Tier 3

An ML model trained on historical payment data automatically flags any transaction that falls below a family's entitled amount. Auto-files a dispute and notifies DTDO — no human needed to detect cheating.

Stack: TensorFlow.js (browser) + Python (server) · isolation forest

Catches 95% of underpayments instantly
F8 🛰️ Satellite + IoT Monitor Tier 3

Sentinel-2 satellite imagery + LoRaWAN soil sensors stream real-time forest health data on-chain. Deforestation alerts trigger automatic investigations. Carbon sequestration gets measured & rewarded.

Stack: Sentinel Hub API · Chainlink oracle · NDVI analysis

Deforestation caught in 24h not 6mo
F9 💰 DeFi Yield + Carbon Credits Tier 3

Idle POL in the cooperative treasury is auto-deposited into Aave to earn yield. Verified carbon credits from the forest are tokenized via Toucan Protocol and sold to corporations — creating a new revenue stream for the community.

Stack: Aave v3 · Toucan · Chainlink Keepers · 4626 vault

₹₹₹ from carbon: 1000-5000 ha/yr
F10 🪪 Self-Sovereign Family DID Tier 4

Each family gets an ERC-725 Decentralized Identifier + Verifiable Credentials issued by DTDO. Works across any app, any chain, any government portal. No more "prove your claim 10 times to 10 agencies".

Stack: ERC-725 / ERC-1056 · W3C VC · Ceramic · social recovery

One identity, infinite interoperability
F11 🌉 Cross-Chain + Govt. Interop Tier 4

LayerZero bridge mirrors claims to Ethereum L1 + L2s for redundancy. Native integrations with DigiLocker, Aadhaar, PM-Kisan, MGNREGA, e-Courts, and State Forest APIs. Bharat-Stack ready.

Stack: LayerZero OFT · Wormhole · UIDAI sandbox · ONDC

Government scheme auto-enrollment
F12 🔐 ZK Privacy Proofs Tier 4

zk-SNARK circuits let families prove "I am over 18", "I belong to a recognized tribe", "I own this claim" without revealing Aadhaar numbers, dates of birth, or full identity. GDPR + DPDP Act 2023 compliant.

Stack: Circom 2 · snarkjs · Groth16 verifier on-chain

Privacy + compliance simultaneously
